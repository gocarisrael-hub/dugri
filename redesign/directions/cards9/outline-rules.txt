Dugri preview: per-design card frame rule

Target: visible frame (card edge to the outer edge of the inner art panel) = 10.78% of card width on the narrow side,
taken from סנטוריני (marriage) and טריפה (tarifa), fronts and backs, which are left exactly as printed.
Tolerance: a card whose side frame is within 9.8-12.3% is left untouched.
Measurement: median over 41 scan lines per side of the first pixel that leaves the frame colour (solid frames);
flood-fill box of the panel for the patterned frames (marriage, tarifa) and the thick-frame templates;
אואזיס fronts use the back's outline box (same Canva template, the orange-slice frame defeats the scan).
Padding: the panel is cut out with a rounded mask (52px radius at 400px, the template corner) and laid on a
flat card of the design's frame colour sized so the side frame is exactly the target; card aspect stays 400x564.
Trim: not needed for any card (no printed frame is thicker than the target + tolerance).
Display: every card gets the same CSS shape: border-radius 6%/4.26%, 1.5px solid black border.
